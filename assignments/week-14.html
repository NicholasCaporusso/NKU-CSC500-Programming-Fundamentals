CSC 500 — Practice Assignments

Week 14: Trees and Core Data-Structure Concepts

The following 50 assignments are based on the Week 14 lecture notes. They collectively cover hierarchical data, tree terminology, binary trees and BSTs, Java references, BST search/insertion/deletion, recursion and traversal, height and complexity, balancing, TreeSet/TreeMap, heaps, priority queues, and data-structure selection.

The assignments deliberately use new trees, values, scenarios, and programming tasks rather than reproducing the worked examples and end-of-lecture questions. Difficulty increases from direct application at Level 1 to multi-concept design and implementation at Level 5.
