CSC 500 — Practice Assignments

Week 6: Classes and Objects in Java

The following 50 assignments are based on the Week 6 lecture. The week emphasizes classes, objects, fields/state, instance methods, constructors, references, null, aliasing, object identity, encapsulation, access modifiers, getters/setters, invariants, this, pass-by-value, arrays of objects, and cooperating objects.

I deliberately avoided recreating the lecture's Student Management System lab, including its GPA/course-tracking and highest-GPA tasks.
